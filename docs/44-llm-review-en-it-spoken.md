# en-it L1 — spoken Italian, not textbook Italian (LLM editorial pass)

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Reviewer:** Claude, LLM review, authorised by the repo owner ·
**Bar:** LLM review plus owner authority — the bar the course shipped on (`tools/course-briefs.ts`,
"en-it: the five decisions"). **No native reviewer has read these edits.** This pass changes what the
learner is asked to say, so it is recorded sentence by sentence; every rewrite reverts by file.

---

## Why

The course's stated goal is speaking. An audit of the 100 hero sentences against how an Italian
actually says them found the register decisions sound (`tu` throughout, `voglio` among friends and
`vorrei` at a counter, `Sono di Delhi`, `Il lunedì lavoro`, `Fa caldo`) and a smaller set of lines
that are grammatical and not said: `Che cosa …?` on eight heroes where speech says `Cosa …?`;
`Voglio tre caffè, per favore` at a counter, where the order is the number and the noun; `Stasera
vado a mangiare` for "going out to eat", which in Italian is `mangio fuori`; `Sono felice oggi`,
where the everyday word is `contento`; `di acqua` where `d'acqua` is what is heard; and a handful
of variations and pool items that were nonsense rather than wrong (`Voglio le pizze`, `Non voglio
un po' di pane`, `Anche io voglio una pizza` after `Voglio un caffè`, `Non mangio perché ho
caldo`). None of the brief's five decisions is withdrawn; one (elision, decision 2) is extended.

## What changed — 14 heroes

**`Cosa`, not `Che cosa`** (6): M5-S07 `Cosa hai mangiato ieri?`, M6-S07 `Cosa fai domani?`,
M10-S02 `Cosa fai domani? Domani lavoro, ma di sera sono libero.`, M10-S09 `Cosa fai stasera? Vado
al cinema con Anna.`, plus every variation, mistake and pool item that opened with it (M5-C03/C08,
M6-C03, M10-C02). The row now displays `Cosa` with forms `Cosa · Che cosa · Che`, so all three
spellings resolve to one note that says `cosa` is what is said and `che cosa` the written form.

**Orders, plans, feelings** (5): M8-S03 `Tre caffè, per favore` (the old variation is the hero;
`Vorrei tre caffè, per favore` stays as the softer variation, `Due pizze, per favore` replaces
`Voglio due pizze, per favore`; `tre` and `per favore` keep their rows); M6-S02 `Stasera mangio
fuori` (new row `fuori`; variations `Stasera mangio a casa`, `Domani mangio fuori con Anna`,
`Stasera non mangio fuori`; mistake now `Stasera sto mangio fuori` — the "am" an English speaker
adds — and the sentence's rules move from andare's to the present-for-plans rule); M9-S04 `Ho sete,
quindi bevo un po' d'acqua` (the old variation is the hero; `Ho sete, quindi bevo una birra` takes
its place); M9-S05 `Oggi sono contento` (row `contento` with forms `contento · contenta · contenti ·
contente · felice`, so `felice` still resolves; `Sei contenta?`, `Anna è contenta oggi`, `Perché sei
contento?` follow); M5-S10 `Stanotte ho dormito bene` (new row `Stanotte` with form `stamattina`,
which is what lets the pool say `Stamattina non ho mangiato`).

**Everyday phrasing** (3): M4-S10 `Parlo con Anna tutti i giorni` (new three-token row `tutti i
giorni`; `ogni giorno` stays on S01 and S07, so the learner hears both); M4-S03 `Di mattina mangio
poco` (new row `poco`, forms `poco · poca · pochi · poche`; the `mangio` row stays on this hero,
which is what a `faccio colazione` hero could not have done — `A che ora mangi?` in the pool needs
`mangi` taught); M2-S10 `Arrivederci!` bare, with `Arrivederci, Anna`, `Grazie, arrivederci` and
`Ciao!` as the variations and a neutral-addressee usage line.

**Cue-side** (2 heroes, 15 cues): M1-S08 `Mi piacciono le canzoni italiane` / "I like Italian
songs" (the audit found "I like songs" an odd thing to say; `le` and `canzoni` had to stay taught,
so the noun kept its article and gained an adjective — `italiani · italiane` are forms on M1-S09's
row); M10-S06 `Anna è italiana e Rohan è indiano. Lei è di Roma, lui è di Delhi.` — the pronouns
now stand where they earn their place, in a two-person contrast, and vanish in the one-person
variation `Anna è italiana, è di Roma.`; rule r1 rewritten to say exactly that. "How much does X
cost?" → "How much is X?" on every price cue (M8, M10); "I had a coffee yesterday"; "I want to get a
coffee"; "I talk to Anna every day"; "So I'm going to get something to eat".

**Variations and pool only:** `d'acqua` in `una bottiglia d'acqua`, `tre bottiglie d'acqua`, `un
po' d'acqua` (M8-S06/S07, M9-S09, M8-C10, M10-C04); `Sabato non ho lavorato` for the one Saturday
(M5-S04, M5-C05; `sabato` is a form on M4's `Il sabato`); `Il pane è in cucina` (M7-S06); `Voglio
due pizze`, `Non voglio pane`, `Non voglio lo zaino`, `Vuole un caffè` (M3); `Oggi non voglio
mangiare` (M3-C07); `Anna vuole un caffè. Anche io voglio un caffè.` (M10-S03 — keeps the one place
`io` belongs and removes the one speaker wanting two things); `Anna è italiana e lavora a Milano`
(M10-C06); `Non mangio perché ho sonno` (M9-C11); `La pizza è buona` / `Le pizze sono buone` for
`La musica è buona` / `I libri sono buoni` (M1-S10), with the `buono` note now saying that music and
books take `bello`.

**Kept on purpose:** everything the audit marked "not a problem" — `tu`, `Buongiorno, Rohan`, `Sono

fuori` stands there. No `bello` row: no M1 noun takes it and no M1 hero can carry it, so M1-C06 is
`Rohan è studente` instead of a sentence with an untaught adjective.

## Mechanics the pass had to respect

- First-occurrence-wins: no row moved between modules. Rows added: `poco` (M4-S03), `tutti i
  giorni` (M4-S10), `Stanotte` (M5-S10), `fuori` (M6-S02). Rows renamed in place: `Che cosa` →
  `Cosa` (M5-S07), `felice` → `contento` (M9-S05). Forms added: `italiani · italiane` (M1-S09),
  `un po'` (M3-S09), `sabato` (M4-S09), `stamattina` (M5-S10), `d'acqua` (M8-S06), `felice`
  (M9-S05), `Che cosa · Che` (M5-S07).
- The elision test (`types.test.ts`, "teaches every apostrophe surface it writes") walks heroes and
  variations too, so `un po' d'acqua` needed both halves taught: `un po'` resolves through M3's
  row (longest match at `un`) and `d'acqua` through M8's `acqua` row. This extends brief decision 2
  — recorded there, dated.
- 17 comprehension-pool items rewritten; every token resolves (the build is the proof; en-it ends
  at 263 surfaces through M10).
- `complexity.allowedPatterns` updated in M4 (`ogni giorno / tutti i giorni`), M5 (`Cosa hai …`),
  M8 (`num + N + , per favore`); rules rewritten in M4 r3, M6 r2, M8 r3/r4, M9 r3, M10 r1.
- `tools/course-briefs.ts`: the en-it patterns for M4, M5 and M8 follow the content; dated
  sentences on the elision decision, the M3 index-seam note, the M5 `Cosa` note, the M6 andare note
  and the M9 `contento` note. No other course's lines touched.
- `src/course/types.test.ts`: untouched — every rewritten line is Latin-only with a straight
  apostrophe.
- `Arrivederci!` is one word against M2's `minWordsPerSentence: 2`; the bound is metadata the
  validator does not enforce, and the one-word goodbye is the line as said.
