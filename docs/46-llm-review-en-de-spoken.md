# en-de L1 — spoken German, not textbook German (LLM editorial pass)

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Reviewer:** Claude, LLM review, authorised by the repo owner ·
**Bar:** LLM review plus owner authority — the bar the course already shipped on (`docs/31`, `docs/33`).
**No native reviewer has read these edits.** This pass changes what the learner is asked to say,
so it is recorded sentence by sentence; every rewrite reverts by file.

---

## Why

The course's stated goal is speaking. An audit of the 100 hero sentences against how a native
speaker actually says them found a handful that were grammatical but never said (`Mir geht es gut,
danke` with nothing handed back, `Ist Ihr Name Weber?`, `Ich wasche mich jeden Tag`, `Ein Kilo
Brot, bitte`, `Eine Tasse Kaffee, bitte`, `Ich bin traurig, weil ich krank bin`), a run of
variations built from words that happened to be in the index rather than from things people say
(`Der Tisch ist in Berlin`, `Das Buch ist neben dem Kaffee`, `Zwölf Äpfel kosten dreißig Euro`),
and one rule that was false: **`dass` is NEVER optional**. After `denken`, `glauben` and `finden`,
spoken German drops `dass` freely and keeps the clause verb-second — `Ich glaube, das Buch ist gut`
— and the plain opinion is `Ich finde das Buch gut`. The course was drilling the one shape it
called the only shape, and marking the commonest spoken one as a mistake.

The register decisions the course was built on are untouched: `Sie` only, umlauts and `ß` written,
one `sie` row, no `glossEn`, Perfekt with `war`/`hatte` in M5, the present for plans in M6 with no
`werden`, `Ich möchte …` at the counter. The audit listed those as not a problem and this pass did
not reopen them.

## What changed — 19 heroes

**A false rule** (M9-S08): `Ich denke, dass das Buch gut ist` → **`Ich finde das Buch gut`**, with
`Ich denke, dass das Buch gut ist` (verb last) and `Ich glaube, das Buch ist gut` (no `dass`, verb
second) as variations and `Ich finde den Kaffee gut` as the third. New rows `finde` (`finden`) and
`glauben` (`glaube`); `dass` and `denken` stay as rows on the same sentence, carried by the
variations. The mistake plate is now the one thing that IS wrong — `Ich denke, dass das Buch ist
gut`, `dass` with the verb left second. Rule r4 rewritten: with `dass` the verb goes last; after
`glauben`/`denken`/`finden` speech drops `dass` and keeps verb-second; both are native. The `dass`
word note and the brief's M9 line say the same.

**`denn`** (M9-S02, S10 and every `denn` line): kept for the V2/V-final contrast, but the cue now
reads "…, for I'm tired (formal)" so it is distinguishable from `weil`, and usage says written /
formal, speech says `weil`. M9-S10 `Ich bin glücklich, denn der Kaffee ist gut` → **`Ich bin froh,
denn der Kaffee ist gut`** (new row `froh`; `glücklich` stays as a row, carried by `Ich bin
glücklich`, and its note now says it is the big word for a life, not a cup of coffee).

**First exchange** (M2, 3 heroes): `Ist Ihr Name Weber?` → **`Sind Sie Frau Weber?`** (the
variation promoted; `Ihr`/`Name` stay as rows on the sentence, carried by `Wie ist Ihr Name?`; new
row `Herr`, carried by `Sind Sie Herr Weber?`; mistake `Ist Sie Frau Weber?` — `Sie` takes the
plural verb). `Ja, ich heiße Weber` → **`Ja, das bin ich`**. `Mir geht es gut, danke` → **`Gut,
danke. Und Ihnen?`** (new two-token row `Und Ihnen`; `Mir geht es` stays as a row, carried by the
variation `Mir geht es gut, danke`, its note saying speech shortens it). `Auf Wiedersehen`, which
the brief names as the course's closing and no display carried, is now a row on M2-S01 with the
variation `Auf Wiedersehen, Frau Weber` and pool item M2-C15.

**My day** (M4, 2 heroes): `Um sieben Uhr esse ich Brot` → **`Um sieben frühstücke ich`** (new row
`frühstücke` · `frühstücken`; `Uhr` stays as a row, carried by `Um sieben Uhr frühstücke ich`,
its note saying speech often drops it). `Ich wasche mich jeden Tag` → **`Ich dusche jeden Tag`**
(new row `dusche` · `duschen`). Decision recorded: `mich` and `wasche` stay as rows on M4-S07,
carried by the variation `Ich wasche mich morgens` and the mistake plate `Ich wasche jeden Tag`
(whose "why" now offers both fixes — add `mich`, or use the verb that needs none). `Ich freue
mich` was considered and rejected: no M4 slot is free without losing a row, and the reflexive
lesson survives intact on the variation and the plate. Rule r6 rewritten to say the everyday
morning verb is not reflexive.

**Tomorrow** (M6, 3 heroes): `Morgen esse ich Brot` → **`Morgen arbeite ich`**; `Übermorgen gehe
ich nach Hause` → **`Übermorgen fahre ich nach Hause`** (new row `fahre` · `fahren`; `gehe` stays
taught by M5's `gegangen` row and `Übermorgen gehe ich nach Hause` is the on-foot variation);
`Ich möchte morgen Deutsch lernen` → **`Morgen lerne ich Deutsch`** (its rules now r0/r1, the
`möchte` version a variation). Rule r0's example moved with S01.

**Where things are** (M7, 1 hero): `Es gibt einen Stuhl` → **`Hier gibt es einen Stuhl`** (new row
`hier`; the `es gibt` row gains the form `gibt es`, which is what the fronting produces and what
`Gibt es hier einen Stuhl?` and M8's `Wie viele Stühle gibt es?` need). The `Straße` row moved
S02 → S07, carried by `Wo ist die Straße?`.

**Numbers & shopping** (M8, 4 heroes): `Wie viele Stühle möchten Sie?` → **`Wie viele Äpfel
möchten Sie?`**; `Ein Kilo Brot, bitte` → **`Ein Kilo Äpfel, bitte`** (with `Ein Brot, bitte` as
the variation — bread is sold by the loaf); `Eine Tasse Kaffee, bitte` → **`Einen Kaffee, bitte`**
(`Tasse` stays as a row, carried by `Eine Tasse Tee, bitte`; the mistake plate is `Ein Kaffee,
bitte`, the dropped accusative -n); `Zwölf Äpfel kosten dreißig Euro` → **`Zwölf Äpfel kosten drei
Euro`** (`dreißig` stays as a row, carried by `Das Buch kostet dreißig Euro`).

**Feelings** (M9, 2 more): `Warum möchten Sie Tee?` → **`Warum sind Sie müde?`**; `Ich bin traurig,
weil ich krank bin` → **`Ich bin müde, weil ich krank bin`** (`traurig` stays as a row, carried by
`Ich bin traurig, weil Anna nicht hier ist` — possible now that `hier` is taught in M7).

**Connected talk** (M10, 2 heroes): `Ich bin müde, aber ich möchte Tee` → **`Ich bin müde, aber ich
arbeite`** (a contrast that is one); `Ich habe Durst. Ich möchte Wasser und Tee.` → **`Ich habe
Durst. Ich möchte Wasser.`** (`und` stays as a row, carried by `Ich habe Hunger und Durst`).

**Variations and pool items rewritten for content** — M1 `Der Tisch ist in Berlin` → `Der Student
ist in Berlin`, `Das Buch ist in Berlin` → `Anna mag das Buch`, `Ein Student ist in Berlin` → `Das
ist ein Student aus Indien`, `Eine Studentin ist in Berlin` → `Das ist eine Studentin aus Berlin`,
`Sie heißen Anna` → `Heißen Sie Anna?`; M3 `Ich esse nicht` → `Ich esse das nicht` (`jetzt` and
`nie` are not taught, `das` is); M7 `Das Buch ist neben/hinter/vor dem Kaffee`, `Der Kaffee ist unter
dem Buch` → `Der Kaffee ist neben dem Buch`, `Das Kino ist neben dem Park`, `Der Park ist hinter dem
Kino`, `Der Stuhl ist unter dem Tisch`, `Der Stuhl ist vor dem Tisch`, `Ich bin vor dem Kino`;
`Der Stuhl ist auf der Straße` → `Ich bin auf der Arbeit`; `Ich gehe auf die Straße` → `Ich gehe
vor die Tür`; M8 `Wie viel kostet ein Kilo Brot?` → `… ein Kilo Äpfel?`; M10 `Warum sind Sie
glücklich?` → `Warum sind Sie froh?`.

**Cues** — M2-S09 `Doch, …` reads "Yes I am, actually — …" on hero, variations and pool; M7-S08
`in den Park` is "to the park" (and pool C10 `zum Park` "heading to the park" so the two stay
apart); M6-S07 and its `nichts machen` lines read "I'd like to do nothing" (`möchte` = would like,
consistently); M9-S04 follows its new hero.

**Kept on purpose:** the audit's "not a problem" list, untouched. `Übermorgen gehe ich nach Hause`
survives as the on-foot variation. `Ich esse jetzt nicht` was not used because `jetzt` is
untaught. `Eine Tasse Kaffee kostet drei Euro` (M8-S07 variation, pool C06) stays: it is a price
list, where the cup is named.

## Mechanics the pass had to respect

- The word index is cumulative and first-occurrence-wins, and `src/course/types.test.ts` forbids
  a second row for any en-de surface. Every row whose hero word disappeared was kept on its sentence
  and carried by a variation (the precedent is M9-S07's `warm` and M8-S10's `billig`), or moved:
  `Straße` M7-S02 → M7-S07. New rows: `Auf Wiedersehen`, `Herr`, `Und Ihnen` (M2), `frühstücke`,
  `dusche` (M4), `fahre` (M6), `hier` (M7), `finde`, `glauben`, `froh` (M9). New form: `gibt es` on
  M7's `es gibt`. `Und Ihnen` is a two-token key beside M10's bare `und`, and `Auf Wiedersehen` a
  two-token key beside M4's bare `auf` — neither collides.
- Every comprehension-pool token must resolve (`checkComprehensionPool`): 19 pool items changed
  (16 rewritten, 3 added — M2-C15, M8-C16, M9-C16); the build is the proof.
- Rules rewritten: M2 r4 (the short answer), M4 r6 (duschen) and r7 (`um sieben`), M6 r0, M7 r0
  (the flip), M8 r3 (the counter order), M9 r0 (`denn` is written), r4 (dass), r5, r6; M10 r1's
  example. `complexity.allowedPatterns` updated in M2, M4, M6, M7, M8, M9.
- `tools/course-briefs.ts`, en-de section only: the M9 `dass` note is marked WITHDRAWN and the M4
  reflexive, M7 `es gibt` and M8 measure-phrase notes marked CHANGED, each dated and pointing here.
  Brief patterns were not edited (they must stay ASCII).
- `src/course/types.test.ts`: no edit needed — no rewritten line violates an assertion.
