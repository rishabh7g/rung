# en-ru L1 — spoken Russian, not textbook Russian (LLM editorial pass)

**Date:** 2026-09-05 · **Reviewer:** Claude, LLM review, authorised by the repo owner ·
**Bar:** LLM review plus owner authority — the bar the course already shipped on (`docs/28`–`30`).
**No native reviewer has read these edits.** This pass changes what the learner is asked to say,
so it is recorded sentence by sentence; every rewrite reverts by file. The worked example it
follows is `docs/39` (the same pass on hi-en).

---

## Why

The course's stated goal is speaking. An audit of the 100 hero sentences against how a Russian
actually says them found one systematic fault and a handful of single ones. The systematic one is
the **repeated `ya`**: `Ya ne rabótayu, potomú chto ya ustál`, `Ya studént i ya rabótayu`,
`Seychás ya rabótayu, potóm ya poydú domóy` — grammatical, and the surest sign of an English
speaker. A Russian names the subject once and the second clause carries on. Two of the first
pass's own brief decisions produced it (M4 and M10, "keep the subject pronoun"), and both are
amended here: the pronoun is still written, but once per sentence.

The single faults: `Ya khochú khleb, pozháluysta` taught as _the_ request when the request has no
verb (`Mne khleb, pozháluysta`); `A vy?` handed back after `Kak delá?` where Russians say `A u
vas?`; a bare perfective on a mass noun (`Vcherá ya výpil kófe`) that sounds unfinished; and a set
of odd or dated content (bread kept in a shop, five-rouble juice, sleeping at night as news).

## Mechanics the pass had to respect

- The word index is cumulative and first-occurrence-wins, so rows were moved or extended, never
  duplicated: `magazíne` S04 → S03 (M7); `dve` now lives on M5-S05 and is gone from M8-S09's
  forms; `U vas` / `U menyá` are forms on the M8 chunk rows; `pyat'` survives as a form on the
  `pyat'desyát` row; `prochitáyu`, `rédko`, `shest'`, `vósem'`, `po-anglíyski` are forms on rows that
  already existed. New rows: `dve`, `cháshki` (M5-S05), `kúkhne` (M7-S04). New rule: M3 r6.
- Every comprehension-pool token must resolve (`checkComprehensionPool`): 13 pool items rewritten
  and two re-cued; the build is the proof. `a potóm` resolves through M10-S02's `A` row.
- `complexity.allowedPatterns`: M3 gains `Mne + N (acc.) + , pozháluysta`, M5 gains
  `Vcherá ya + V-l + num + N (gen.)`.
- `tools/course-briefs.ts`, en-ru section only: M3, M4, M5, M8 and M10 notes carry a dated
  amendment pointing here (pronoun once; the request shape; `dve cháshki`; the two-token `U`
  forms). No other course's lines were touched.
- `src/course/types.test.ts`: the en-ru block is unchanged — every rewritten line is Latin
  `display`, Cyrillic `script`, precomposed acutes.
- `script` and `display` were rewritten together on every edited sentence, variation, mistake, word
  row and pool item; no `glossEn` anywhere; `vy` throughout.
